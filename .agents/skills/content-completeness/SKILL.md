# Content Completeness Skill

This skill provides guidelines and validation criteria to ensure every website in RackerWeb possesses comprehensive, authentic, and sector-accurate content.

## Core Rule: Substantial Content, Zero Placeholders
A website must never look like an empty template waiting for content. Content must be directly authored, complete, and fully populated.

### Prohibited Tells:
- "Lorem ipsum" or generic filler text.
- Repeated descriptions across different products (e.g. 10 cakes all described as "Bánh thơm ngon làm từ nguyên liệu tươi").
- Single-paragraph "About Us" pages.
- Services listed without pricing, duration, scope, or procedure.
- Articles/news posts that are only 2–3 sentences long.
- Contact pages with nothing but a bare form.

---

## 1. Content Density Thresholds by Page Type

### A. Homepage (`index.html`)
- Minimum word count: **800 – 1,800 words**.
- Must feature at least **8 distinct content sections**.
- Must include tangible business data: real pricing, operating hours, exact physical address, warranties, and specific service steps.

### B. Product & Service Catalog (`san-pham.html`)
- For e-commerce/retail stores: **12 – 24 distinct items**.
- For service/agency/clinic websites: **8 – 16 comprehensive service packages**.
- Each item MUST have:
  - Specific, non-generic title.
  - Transparent price or pricing formula.
  - Estimated duration or delivery turnaround.
  - 3–4 step execution scope.
  - Target audience suitability note ("Phù hợp nhất với...").
  - Post-service home care / usage instructions.

### C. About Us / Methodology (`gioi-thieu.html`)
- Minimum word count: **600 – 1,200 words**.
- Must detail:
  - Brand founding story and historical context (fictional but authentic).
  - Operating philosophy and core values.
  - Team member profiles (minimum 3 key practitioners with background, role, and specialties).
  - Material standards, equipment specs, and safety/sterilization protocols.

### D. Booking & Contact Desk (`lien-he.html`)
- Form with complete input validation (name, phone, service picker, date picker, time slot, notes).
- Clear customer preparation guidance ("Trước khi đến cần chuẩn bị gì").
- Operating schedules with explicit holiday / closure notes.
- Emergency / hotline contact box with Zalo consultation bridge.
- Local draft storage status and export workflow.

## 2. Industry Content Authenticity Guidelines
When authoring content for specific industries:
- **Medical / Dental**: Reference real clinical equipment (e.g. *Papaya 3D Plus CBCT Scanner*, *Ectoin 2%*, *Ceramide 3-6-9*, *Clear Aligners*).
- **Automotive Engineering**: Detail diagnostic steps (ECU scan, brake pad caliper tolerances, 3D Hunter wheel alignment, Meguiar's dual-action detailing).
- **Artisan Grooming**: Specify scissor slicing techniques, hot towel temperatures (70°C), sandalwood essential oils, and skull shape anatomy.
- **Architecture & Timber Joinery**: Detail timber species (American Black Walnut, White Oak, Teak), moisture content (<10% MC), joinery techniques (Dovetail, Bowtie key, Mortise & Tenon).
